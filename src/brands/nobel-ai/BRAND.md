## Motion character

Energetic and precise. Elements spring in with a slight stagger; the background
drifts slowly so frames never feel static. Confident, not frantic.

## Voice on screen

Direct and capability-forward — what the product does for the business, in plain
words. Short lines. No jargon, no hype adjectives.

## Audience

Business owners and operators who want AI to remove manual work. Context clues
from existing files suggest AI automation and GoHighLevel dashboards.
