# Your Best Address (YBA) — video brand brief

The authoritative source for YBA is the **`yba-brand-system` skill**, plus
`yba-write-content` for copy and `yba-social-director` for planning. This file
covers only what those skills don't: how YBA translates into motion.

## Hard rules that apply to video

- `#53655c` is the dominant surface in every frame. Never black, never a dark
  background as the main surface.
- No gradients (beyond barely-perceptible), no glows, no neon.
- **Max 10 words on screen at once.** Count them per scene, not per video.
- One idea per scene. Generous margins. If a frame feels busy, remove something.

## Motion character

Calm and unhurried. Slow fades and gentle rises — nothing bouncy, nothing that
snaps. Hold each scene long enough to read twice. Cuts should feel like turning
a page, not like a transition effect.

## Voice on screen

Knowledgeable friend, not vendor. Explain, don't sell. No urgency, no countdowns,
no "don't miss out." Closing lines are soft offers of help, e.g.
"Happy to walk you through it."

## Audience

People choosing a freer, simpler life: RV travelers, digital nomads, expats,
remote professionals, and small business owners who want privacy.

## Assets

Drop real files in `public/brands/yba/` and reference them from `brand.ts`:

- `logo.png` or `logo.svg` — the YBA mark
- `lifestyle/` — RV, laptop, mail, and travel photos for Lifestyle Overlay scenes
- `fonts/` — real brand fonts, if they differ from the Helvetica fallback
